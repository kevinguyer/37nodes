# Tags and starred items

Lightweight ways to group things that do not live under the same parent.

## Tags

Any `#word` in a title or note is a tag, shown as a clickable chip. Click one to filter the outline to it, and click it again to clear.

Typing `#` offers the tags you already use, most-used first, so a vocabulary stays a vocabulary instead of drifting into `#todo`, `#todos` and `#to-do`.

The panel's **Tags** section lists every tag in view with a count of the items using it. With **Hide completed** on, the count covers only items you can see, and a tag used only on finished items drops out of the list (it is still offered when you type `#`).

## Starred items

`Alt+S` stars the item you are on, or use ★ in the panel or the ≡ menu. A star marks the item at the right edge of its row, and the panel's **Starred** section keeps every starred item one click away, wherever it lives in the outline. It is a shortlist you curate, in document order.

## Headings and colour

The ≡ menu gives an item a heading size (H1, H2, H3) and a bullet colour from a six-colour palette. Both are purely visual and do not change the structure.
