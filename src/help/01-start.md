# Getting started

An outline is a list where every item can hold its own list, as deep as you like. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

## The basics

- Click an item's text and type. There is no edit mode: the outline is always editable.
- `Enter` makes a new item below. `Tab` indents it under the item above; `Shift+Tab` brings it back out.
- Click a bullet to **zoom** into that item and make it the whole page. The breadcrumbs at the top lead back out.
- `Shift+Enter` adds a **note** under an item: longer text that supports markdown.
- `Ctrl+Z` undoes, about 200 steps back, including structural changes.

## Find your way around

- `Ctrl+K` jumps to any item by typing a few letters of its title.
- `Ctrl+F` searches everything in view.
- `Alt+I` opens the **info panel**: what links to the current item, your starred items, and every tag you use.
- The **☰ menu** holds themes, export and import, and these help pages.
